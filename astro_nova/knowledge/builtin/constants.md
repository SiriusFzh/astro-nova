## 常用换算
- 1 eV = 1.602176634 × 10^{-19} J
- 1 Jy (央斯基) = 10^{-26} W m^{-2} Hz^{-1}
- 1 erg = 10^{-7} J
- 1 dyn = 10^{-5} N
- 1 Gauss = 10^{-4} T
- 1 pc = 3.0857 × 10^{16} m
- 1 kpc = 1000 pc
- 1 Mpc = 10^6 pc
- 温度换算: T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15
